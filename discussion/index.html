Discussion

We will have four discussion sessions. Each discussion session is centered around one article.

Please read the article prior to discussion session and reply to the Ed thread answering a few questions about the reading. Please try to make your posts by noon on the day before discussion. We encourage you to not only post your own responses, but to reply to your classmates’ responses as well. We’ll use these responses as a starting point for our discussion.

In the discussion session, we will first split the class to smaller groups and discuss among themselves. Then we will ask everyone to talk in-turn.
